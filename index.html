The question

All secondary students should be required to complete community service before graduating. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
  • Task 2
  • Opinion essay
  • 250+ words
  • 40 minutes
01

Initial
Draft

The first attempt, written before any feedback. Mistakes included.

Draft 1Word count: 289

Many educators and policy makers believe community service should be a compulsory requirement before middle and high school pupils graduate. Although this policy is driven by positive intentions, I firmly disagree as requiring civic action would contradict the voluntarism aspect of this task and bring burden to the respective host organization.

The primary objective of community service is to voluntarily act on civic activities. By definition, community service is an act of self involvement to contribute to society. If the education department were to turn community service into a mandatory prerequisite for students to graduate, that action could and will spark an assignment feeling in pupils, turning community service as an act of voluntarism into a checkbox for scholars to list off, further contradicting the whole point of community service.

Furthermore, host organizations that civic actions are usually targeted by typically have limited amounts of time, resources and staff. If community services are compulsory, this would lead to other parties needing to spare valuable time and space which could be used to develop something much more productive for their organizations. Staff members would also need to supervise students doing these acts of self involvement thus bringing more burden towards the committees of the organization.

Finally, most secondary school students already have involvement in their own society or school environment. Some of which include the student council, charity clubs and more. Converting community service into a requirement would make an impact on students' academic activities due to stress.

In conclusion, although community services leaves a good impact on the society and organization scholars are trying to help, turning it into a prerequisite would leave more negative than positive impacts towards the society, thus leaning me into disagreeing with the notion.

Draft 2Word count: 270

A number of educators and policy makers believe community service should be a compulsory requirement before middle and high school pupils graduate. Although this policy is driven by positive intentions, I firmly disagree that requiring civic action would contradict the voluntarism aspect of this task and place a burden on respective host organizations.

The primary objective of community service is to participate voluntarily in civic activities. By definition, community service is an act of self involvement to contribute to society. If the education department were to turn community service into a mandatory prerequisite for students to graduate, this policy would spark an assignment mindset in pupils. The students might view community service as a mere checklist for scholars to complete, further contradicting the whole point of community service.

Furthermore, host organizations accommodating student volunteers typically have limited time, resources and staff. If community service were to become compulsory, this would divert other parties to spare valuable time and space which could be used to develop something much more productive for their organizations. Staff members would also need to supervise students during these mandatory tasks thus placing more burden towards the committees of the organization.

Finally, most secondary school students already have involvement in their own society or school environment. Some of which include the student council, charity clubs and more. Converting community service into a requirement would make an impact on students' academic activities due to stress.

In conclusion, although community service impacts the society in a positive way, turning it into a prerequisite would leave more negative impacts towards the society, thus leaning me into disagreeing with the notion.

What I changed

  • Replaced “Many” with “A number of”
  • Made verbs and phrases more natural and formal
  • Rewrote “assignment feeling” as “assignment mindset”
  • Fixed community service / services agreement
  • Clarified who the host organisations are
03

Final
Essay

The polished version, after feedback and self-editing.

Intro

A number of educators and policymakers believe that unpaid civic work should be a compulsory requirement before high school pupils graduate. Although this policy is driven by positive intentions, I firmly disagree because making volunteering mandatory contradicts its altruistic nature and places an unnecessary burden on host organizations.

Body 1

The primary objective of community service is to participate voluntarily in civic activities. By definition, community service is an act of voluntary participation to contribute to society. If the education department were to turn community service into a mandatory prerequisite for students to graduate, this policy would spark an assignment mindset in pupils. The students might view community service as a mere checklist for scholars to complete, further contradicting the whole point of community service.

Body 2

Furthermore, host organizations accommodating student volunteers typically have limited time, resources and staff. If community service were to become compulsory, this would divert other parties to spare valuable time and space which could be used to develop something much more productive for their organizations. Staff members would also need to supervise students during these mandatory tasks thus placing more burden towards the committees of the organization.

Body 3

Finally, forcing outreach hours overlooks the actual contributions students already make while adding unnecessary academic pressure. Many young individuals actively support their communities through student councils, charity clubs, and peer mentoring. Forcing them to work additional hours outside of school risks inducing burnout and detracting from their core studies, particularly for those preparing for high-stakes university entrance exams.

Conclusion

In conclusion, although community service impacts the society in a positive way, turning it into a prerequisite would leave more negative impacts towards the society, thus leaning me into disagreeing with the notion.
